💡 How to get these credentials?

  1. Register/Login at the JazzCash Sandbox Portal.
  2. Go to the **Credentials Manager** or **Sandbox settings** inside the dashboard.
  3. Set your redirection **Return URL** to: http://localhost:3000/payment/callback.
  4. Generate and copy your **Merchant ID**, **Password**, and **Integrity Salt**.
  5. Paste them above and save them locally to start testing transaction redirection!
